# Privacy Policy (draft - have it reviewed before publishing)

_Last updated: <date>. Applies to the Agent Khata Android app
(`com.nextgenai.agentkhata`)._

## Summary

Agent Khata keeps your business records **on your phone**. We do not run a
server that receives your records. The app uses the internet only to show
advertisements from Google AdMob (see "Advertising" below); your business
records are never part of an ad request.

## What the app stores

On your phone only:

- Accounts and balances you enter, transactions you record, commission rates,
  expenses, day-close results and an audit log of changes.
- Optional customer phone numbers and names you type, and (only if you turn
  on "Remember customers") a list used to suggest them.
- Settings such as language and display options.
- A PIN (stored only as a salted hash). The database is encrypted with a key
  held in the Android Keystore.

## Advertising

The app shows banner and full-screen ads served by **Google AdMob**.

- Ads are **not personalized**. Every ad request asks for non-personalized ads,
  and the app does not request the Android advertising ID.
- Ad requests do not contain your transactions, balances, customer numbers,
  names, notes or any other business record.
- To deliver and measure ads, Google's SDK may process technical information
  such as your IP address, device and app information and diagnostics. See
  Google's privacy policy for details: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Where the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent form, and
  Settings > About > "Ad privacy options" lets you review your choice.
- Ads never appear on screens where you enter money, PINs or counts.

## What leaves the phone

Apart from ad requests described above, nothing, unless **you** choose to:

- **Back up**: you create an encrypted backup file and decide where to send
  it (for example Google Drive or WhatsApp). The file is encrypted with the
  passphrase you choose. We cannot read it or recover the passphrase.
- **Share a daily summary** as an image or PDF.
- **Share a diagnostics report** with support. It contains technical facts
  (version, database size, health check, error log, anonymous usage counts if
  you opted in). It does not contain your transactions, amounts or customer
  details, but error logs can occasionally include technical text; review it
  before sending.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet and network state | Load advertisements |
| Notifications | Daily "close the day" reminder and low-balance alerts |
| Biometrics / fingerprint | Optional fingerprint/face unlock |
| Vibrate | Haptic feedback when an entry is saved or a notification arrives |
| Receive boot completed | Re-schedule the daily reminder after a restart |

The app does not request contacts, SMS, location, camera, microphone or
the advertising ID.

## Your choices

- Turn off reminders, biometrics, customer suggestions and usage counts in
  Settings at any time.
- Uninstalling the app deletes the data on the phone. Backup files you
  created remain wherever you saved them.

## Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.
