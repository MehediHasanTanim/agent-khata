## English

**Title (30):** Agent Khata: Cash Book

**Short description (80):** Daily cash book for mobile banking and flexiload shops. Faster than paper.

**Full description:**

Put the paper notebook away.

Agent Khata is a cash book for mobile financial services agents and
flexiload retailers. It works fully offline.

- Record cash in, cash out, flexiload, float purchases and expenses in a few taps
- Always see your cash and each wallet's balance
- Commission is calculated automatically (you set the rates)
- Close the day in a minute and get hints about where a gap may be
- Void or correct entries; the history is kept
- Protected by PIN and fingerprint; your data stays on your phone
- Encrypted backups you can restore on a new phone
- Bangla and English, with Bangla digits

Note: this is a record-keeping app only. It does not move money and is not an
app of, or affiliated with, any mobile financial service or mobile operator.
